# 06 — Naming

Working name: **Noggin**. Change it when something better survives the criteria below.

## Criteria

1. Says "brain / mind / self-knowledge", not "content" or "AI". The product is the knowing, not the generating.
2. One or two syllables. Works as a verb or a possessive ("open your Noggin", "it's in my Noggin").
3. British-friendly, warm, slightly playful. Not clinical, not Silicon Valley.
4. Doesn't collide with a known AI product. (Avoid anything with "mind", "brain", "synapse", "cortex" as the whole name; they're crowded.)
5. A clean `.co.uk` or `.com` is available, or a short compound is (`getnoggin.com`, `noggin.app`).

## Candidates

| Name | Why it might work | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Noggin** | Warm, British, self-deprecating in the right way. "What's in your noggin?" is the whole pitch | A learning platform of the same name exists in the US; check trademark class |
| **Lobe** | Short, precise, maps to the visual (regions are lobes). "Your opinions lobe has gone quiet" | Slightly cold; also a Microsoft ML product name |
| **Grey Matter** | Memorable, literal, owns the grey-with-tinted-lobes look | Two words; a few agencies use it |
| **Spine** | Already the word for the four core stories; strong, structural | Loses the brain metaphor |
| **Inkling** | A half-formed idea, which is what people arrive with | Common word, crowded |
| **Headroom** | Room to think; suggests space and growth | Less about self-knowledge |
| **Recall** | What the product does: pulls what you already know | Generic |

Front-runner: Noggin, unless the trademark check fails, then Grey Matter.

## Do before committing

- Companies House and UKIPO trademark search (class 42, class 9)
- Domain check: `.co.uk`, `.com`, `.app`
- Say it out loud in the sentence "I'm using ___ for my LinkedIn" and in "it's on my ___ board"
- Check the name doesn't read badly as a LinkedIn headline suffix
